# Sock Only — refined bold Facebook ad kit

This revision returns to the original blue-and-white creative direction. It keeps the prominent product, blue circle and bold “Boring socks? Not today.” hook. Changes are limited to type hierarchy, spacing and a simpler footer. The plain catalog direction is superseded.

## Download

- **sockonly-feed-ad.png** — refined still ad with the full logo, mixed-case hook, large socks, product title, compact CTA and website. AI-assisted artwork derived from the supplied references; verify product/logo fidelity against approved assets before use.
- **sockonly-reels-1080x1920.mp4** — 10 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, H.264, silent. Blue-circle composition with the existing logo/product assets, small controlled motion and one fixed hook. No cycling through extra slogans.

- **render-video.mjs** — editable rendering source. Requires Node, Playwright, Chromium and FFmpeg. Run `node marketing/facebook/render-video.mjs` with dependencies installed.

## Suggested Facebook copy

**Primary text**

Boring socks? Not today. Meet Mystic Clouds, limited-run crew socks from Sock Only.

**Headline:** Meet Mystic Clouds

**Meta CTA:** Shop Now

**Destination:** The verified, published Mystic Clouds product page. Confirm that `https://sockonly.com/products/mystic-clouds` is live, not password-protected and supports real checkout before using it.

## Placement and review

Use the MP4 for Reels/Stories and inspect the still in the Feed placement preview. Check all placement crops, overlay zones and logo/product legibility in Meta Ads Manager before publishing. Check the still’s actual crop in the selected placement; the vertical video is 1080 × 1920.

No price, discount, stock count, shipping promise, fake review or conversion claim is included. No campaign has been published, no budget set, and no money spent. Ad/website consistency, stock and checkout readiness remain merchant checks.

The video intentionally has no audio. If adding music or narration, use appropriately licensed material. For a more natural photographic campaign later, replace the supplied mockup with approved real flat-lay or on-foot photography/video. No generated people or fabricated customer testimonials are used here.

## Measuring the change

Compare the clean creative with the previous version while keeping audience, destination, offer and budget allocation consistent. Assess purchases and revenue alongside click metrics. An aesthetic revision is not evidence of increased conversion.
